Endless Paper

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how the Endless Paper app for Android ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is published by druidarun (the developer).

Summary

  • The app has no accounts and no servers. We do not collect your name, email, location or contacts.
  • Your drawings and images stay on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device information to serve ads.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your sketches (strokes, colors, shapes and the position of images) in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. When you add an image or SVG file, the app copies it into the same private storage so it is still available the next time you open the app. We do not upload this content anywhere and we cannot see it.

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account, according to your device settings. That backup is handled by Google and not by us.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information ourselves. We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own, and we do not sell personal information.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show and measure ads, and to help prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (for example model, operating system version and app version), and how you interact with ads. Google may use this information to show personalized ads, where this is allowed.

To learn how Google handles this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the AdMob and Google Play data disclosure.

You can control ad personalization in your Android settings under Google, then Ads, where you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization. The exact menu names depend on your device.

Permissions

The app asks for network access only so that ads can load. You choose images and SVG files with Android's own file picker, so the app does not need permission to read your photos or storage. The app does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your sketches stay on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
